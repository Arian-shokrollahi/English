# 01-Why do you choose Linux?
---
## جمله خودم که نوشتمش
- “why you are choose linux? I learn Linux because it’s incredibly powerfull and flexibile. And really enjoy when I work with terminal I really like terminal environment and its give me good feel”
- مشکلاتش چیه::

- ساختار سوالی: در انگلیسی برای سوالی کردن، باید از فعل کمکی استفاده کنی. به جای why you are choose باید بگویی Why do you choose یا Why did you choose.
املای کلمات (Spelling): کلمه powerfull باید powerful باشد (یک l در آخر) و flexibile باید flexible باشد.
ساختار جمله (Sentence Structure): جمله دوم کمی طولانی و پشت سر هم است. بهتر است از نقطه یا حروف ربط درست استفاده کنی.
حرف اضافه و مالکیت: به جای its give me good feel باید بگویی it gives me a good feeling.

--- 
- جمله ی درسته
  
- I want to learn Linux because it is incredibly powerful and flexible. I really enjoy working with terminal; I prefer its command-line environment over a graphical user interface. My ultimate goal is to become a skilled Linux administrator and network engineer. Currently, I am using WSL(windows subsystem Linux) to practice my commands and explore the system.
